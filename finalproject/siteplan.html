Explore Myrtle Beach - Site Plan

Site Name

Explore Myrtle Beach

This name was chosen because the website focuses on highlighting attractions, activities, and important information about Myrtle Beach for visitors and potential business members. It clearly communicates the purpose of discovery and tourism.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to promote Myrtle Beach by providing visitors with information about local attractions, events, dining, and business opportunities. It also serves as a resource for individuals interested in joining the Chamber of Commerce.

Scenarios

  • What are the top attractions to visit in Myrtle Beach?
  • Where can I find local businesses to support or join?
  • What events are happening this week in the area?

Color Scheme

  • #1d3557 (Dark Blue) – Headings, navigation, and important elements
  • #457b9d (Blue) – Secondary accents, buttons, and highlights
  • #f1faee (Light Background) – Page background for readability

Typography

  • Montserrat – Used for headings to create a bold and modern look
  • Open Sans – Used for body text for readability and clean design

Wireframe

Mobile View

The mobile layout stacks content vertically with a simple navigation menu, hero image, and content sections for easy scrolling.

Desktop View

The desktop layout expands into multiple columns with a navigation bar at the top, a hero section, and content arranged in a grid for better use of space.

Additional Planning Considerations

  • Responsive design will be implemented using CSS Grid and Flexbox.
  • Accessibility will be considered using proper semantic HTML and alt text.
  • Performance optimization will include compressed images and efficient CSS.
  • SEO best practices will include proper headings and meta tags.
